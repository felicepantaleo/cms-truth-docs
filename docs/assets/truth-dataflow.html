Truth Graph Data Flow

The modules that build the MC-truth graph at DIGI, keep it in the output, and use it at RECO to associate and validate the reconstruction. Module labels, types and inputs come from the configuration of the runTheMatrix workflow 34834.88 (TTbar, D122, no pile-up) in the IB. Select a module to see what it reads, what reads it, its main parameters, its open items and the documentation.

How to turn each part on

  1. Build and keep the graph. Nothing to do in a Run4 workflow whose era applies enableTruth (the C17 eras). Premix and FastSim do not build it. Other eras: --procModifiers enableTruth.
  2. Graph summary and comparison with the legacy truth. On by default in the validation step under enableTruth: truthGraphSummaryValidator, branchHGCalValidator, branchTrackingValidator.
  3. Association maps and their plots. --customise SimGeneral/TruthGraphAssociatorProducers/customiseTruthGraphAssociators.customiseTruthBranchValidation on the RECO and the HARVESTING steps, or the .88 workflows of runTheMatrix.
  4. Association maps in the output file. customiseTruthGraphAssociators.customiseTruthGraphAssociators.
  5. HLT collections. customiseTruthGraphAssociators.customiseTruthHltValidation.
  6. Run metadata. --customise PhysicsTools/TruthInfo/customiseTruthMetadata.addTruthMetadata (#52350, not in the IB).
